Nova

A voice assistant that runs entirely on an Android phone, with no server and no internet. A small fine-tuned language model reads what you said and proposes an action. A safety gate checks that action before anything happens on the phone.

The live console runs the app's own code, model and gate on the project PC, so it only opens on the campus network. In the console the tools are pretend: nothing is called or sent.

The model can never act on its own. Every call it proposes goes through ToolGate: the arguments are validated against the tool catalog, calls, messages and payments need a spoken “yes” (read from a closed word list, never by the model), and Android permissions are checked. A small BERT classifier says “say it another way” when a request matches no tool at all.

What it can do

Everyday phone tasks

Alarms, named timers, reminders, calls and messages (contacts matched by sound), calendar, notes, calculator, opening apps and settings.

Follow-ups and undo

“What about tomorrow?”, “aur kal ka?”, “undo that”. A failed-commands screen lets you teach Nova.

Screenshot → calendar

Share a meeting screenshot: on-device OCR fills in the event, and you always review it before saving.

Safety

QR codes that say who they pay, hide-and-share for private details, and a local-only switch with a log of every network request.

Household

A ledger for household helpers (visits and the monthly bill) and gas/water refill forecasts.

Fall detection + SOS

A drop, a hard hit, then lying still at a new angle. Nova asks “Are you OK?”; with no answer in 45 s it texts and calls your contact.

Fall detection, without AI

Physics instead of a model: a drop below 0.6 g, a hit above 2.2 g, then lying still turned by at least 65°. Jogging hits 4–5 g many times but never lies still afterwards, so it never fires.

Install on a phone

Android 8 or newer (tested on Android 15) with an ARM64 chip that supports dot-product instructions. The app holds no models; they are copied to its files folder once (about 390 MB).

adb install -r nova-release.apk
D=/sdcard/Android/data/dev.nova.assistant/files
adb push nova_q8_ekv3072_p64.litertlm ggml-base.en-q5_1.bin ggml-silero-v6.2.0.bin $D/
adb push gate.json vocab.txt gate_fp16.onnx $D/gate/     # optional tool gate

Open Nova from the launcher, wait for “Model READY”, then type, or tap or hold to talk.
